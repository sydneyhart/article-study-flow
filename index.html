YOUR WEEKLY READING COMPANION

Read deeply. Summarize clearly.

Turn a spreadsheet of readings into consistent, source-checkable study notes.

1. Add your reading list

Export Excel or Google Sheets as CSV UTF-8. Required: url. Optional: title, text, sensitive (yes/no). Up to 25 articles.

Download test dataset

2. Choose how to summarize

Local AI sends article text only to Ollama at 127.0.0.1. Use an installed local model, not a cloud model. Public retrieval contacts article websites and reveals your IP and requested URL. For sensitive readings, supply text and set sensitive=yes; those rows are never fetched. No analytics, cloud export, or saved history.

Ready when you are.

3. Review & share

Check every draft against its reading. Evidence excerpts are checked against the supplied text; interpretation still needs your review.

Download HTML to share or print to PDF. To use Google Docs, upload the HTML file to Drive and open it with Google Docs. Review sharing permissions before sending sensitive notes.

Built with AI-assisted iterative coding. No account or Python packages required.
